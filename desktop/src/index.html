    Or paste rows copied from Excel

    One row per departure. Columns: Time (HH:MM), Flight, To, Zone (A, B or C), Pax (expected passengers) and optional Type (short, lowcost or long: how early people come to the airport). English or Macedonian headers are recognised and the column order does not matter. Nothing leaves this computer.
